# Round three, claude hand

Three grids drawn by hand for build 156. Print them with
`python3 docs/logo/round3/render.py docs/logo/round3/claude/design-N.txt`, or add `--plain` for
the shape alone.

**Design 1, the rearing hydra.** 52 by 40 pixels, 20 terminal rows. This is the whole animal
standing up the way charizard stands in the reference. The middle head faces you with its jaws
open and two horns. The outer heads turn away in profile and snap outward, each with a row of
teeth. Three necks come down with bone spines and meet in one gold-bellied chest. The body has
two clawed feet and a tail that curls up on the right with a spade tip. It uses the mascot's
emerald and gold, with orange-red eyes, so it reads as the same dragon as the one in the Herdr
pane, just grown up and angrier.

**Design 2, the crest.** 52 by 42 pixels, 21 rows, mirror-symmetric. Three heads face you
straight on, each with swept horns, a slanted brow over yellow eyes, and a mouth open wide
enough to show both rows of fangs. The middle head stands higher than the others. Their necks
come down into a broad chest with a gold belly, like the top of a coat of arms. I went crimson
and near-black here because rounds one and two were all green and polite, and a red hydra
with its teeth out is the opposite of polite.

**Design 3, the banner.** 65 by 20 pixels, 10 rows, the design with the word. A small violet
hydra on the left: three heads with cyan eyes, the middle one with its mouth open, a lilac body
over an amber belly. `hydra` is lettered beside it in two-pixel strokes, painted from pink at
the top to yellow at the bottom, with a dark purple drop shadow down and to the right. This is
the one sized for a CLI to print at startup: short enough to sit above a prompt, and under 80
columns wide.
